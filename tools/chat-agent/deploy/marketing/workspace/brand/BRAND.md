# Homebase brand

From `Homebase_designmanual_v2.pdf` (Dec 2024). The manual wins if this file disagrees.

## Idea

The logo echoes the Big Dipper and the North Star: Homebase as a compass toward privacy, freedom and self-determination. Colors come from the Northern Lights: wonder and protection.

## Colors

| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Primary purple | `#603E90` | logo, main brand color |
| Primary blue | `#425985` | logo, main brand color |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | backgrounds |
| Black | `#000000` | text |
| Secondary blue | `#476A92` | variety, depth |
| Secondary green | `#00966D` | variety, depth (aurora green) |
| Accent magenta | `#EB00EB` | small parts only: highlights, interactive bits |

Primary and secondary colors also come at 50% tints.

## Typography

- **Headings and logotype:** Montserrat Alternates. Web headlines mix light and bold, and start lowercase (no leading capital).
- **Body:** Montserrat. Regular for body text, light for paragraph headings.
- **Long text:** Open Sans.
- Files in `fonts/`; they are also installed in the container, so HTML can use them by name.

## Logo

- `logos/homebase_org_rgb_2.svg`: the mark. `logos/homebase_logotype_2.svg`: mark plus "homebase.id" for light backgrounds; `logos/homebase_logotype_negativ_2.svg` for dark ones.
- Keep a clear margin around it equal to the width of the leg of the "h".
- Never smaller than 48 px on screen.
- Use the files as they are: don't redraw, recolor or re-space the logotype.

## Photos

`photos/` holds royalty-free Pexels images the brand already uses; new ones come from pexels.com too. Look for a modern, Nordic, minimalist style: homes, people of diverse ages and genders enjoying privacy at home, nature with purple-green tones. If unsure whether a color works, go black and white.
